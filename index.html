05 // About

Beyond Pixels & Codes

I design digital products with a single conviction: simplicity is the highest sophistication. Operating at the intersection of complex logic and visual harmony, I craft interfaces that feel intuitive, human, and responsive. I focus on translating intricate user requirements into clean grid hierarchies, intentional typography, and micro-animations that deliver high-delight outcomes.

Currently, I lead the UI/UX design efforts at MediSetu, designing patient-facing healthcare applications that simplify clinical consultation and patient onboarding. By structuring interfaces that respect cognitive limits, I bridge medical workflows with user accessibility.

Outside the canvas, I am deeply curious about design history, typography systems, and frontend logic. You will often find me exploring local cafes in Jaipur, curating aesthetic setups, reading design books, or getting competitive with new interaction design patterns. I believe that good designers are empathetic observers, always looking to bring positive, constructive vibes to any table.

06 // FAQ

Your questions, answered

A quick dive into my design philosophy, creative principles, and workflows.

I believe that simplicity is the highest sophistication. I focus on reducing cognitive load, creating strict typography hierarchies, utilizing fluid spacing, and designing intentional micro-interactions that make interfaces feel alive and human.

I design with high engineering feasibility in mind. Having experience with frontend technologies (HTML, CSS, JS, Framer), I deliver detailed design handoffs in Figma with clear responsive rules, states, variables, and animations, making translation clean and painless.

I primarily use Figma for interactive component logic, Framer for publishing production-level layouts, and Spline for 3D interactions. I love prototyping micro-interactions early in the design phase to validate transitions.
